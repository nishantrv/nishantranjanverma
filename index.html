About

About Me

Most AI demos work. Far fewer survive their first month with real users. I have spent eight years on the gap between the two.

That work has taken me through Google, Adobe, Wayfair and Virgin Media, and most recently Solventum (3M Healthcare) — across healthcare, telecommunications, e-commerce and supply chain.

Today it means multi-agent graphs in LangGraph, retrieval pipelines that can show their sources, MCP integrations, and the evaluation, tracing and cost controls that decide whether a feature ships or quietly gets rolled back. Underneath that sits eight years of classical ML and data engineering — recommendation and forecasting models running at scale, MLOps pipelines, and the warehouse work that has to exist before any of it is possible.

I write about this on Medium, usually about the parts nobody puts in a demo. If you are building something in this space, I would like to hear about it.
